White Desert camping: sleep between the chalk rocks of Bahariya

This is real desert camping, not a hotel garden with tents. We drive you by 4x4 from Bahariya Oasis deep into the White Desert reserve, set a private camp among the giant chalk mushrooms, cook dinner on an open fire, and you fall asleep to a sky with more stars than you have ever seen.

What the camping trip looks like

Day 1 - Cairo → Bahariya → camp

  • 07:00 · Pickup from your hotel in Cairo, drive south (4-5 hrs with a coffee stop)
  • 11:30 · Bahariya Oasis, switch to the 4x4, lunch
  • 13:00 · Black Desert volcanic hills and the cold spring
  • 15:00 · Crystal Mountain and the golden cliffs of Agabat Valley
  • 17:00 · Sunset between the chalk formations of the White Desert
  • 19:00 · Bedouin dinner by the campfire, stargazing, sleep in camp

Day 2 - Sunrise at camp → Mushroom Rock → Cairo

  • 06:00 · Sunrise between the white rocks, breakfast at camp
  • 08:00 · Mushroom and Chicken Rock, the Valley of the Tents
  • 09:30 · Sandboarding on the dunes
  • 11:00 · Drive back toward Bahariya, lunch at the oasis
  • 17:00 · Drop-off at your Cairo hotel

The full program with photos is on the 2-day safari page, and the short-notice option is on the overnight from Cairo page.

The camp itself

You sleep in a proper tent with mattresses and thick blankets, set up just for your group. Dinner is cooked on the fire - grilled chicken, rice, vegetables, Bedouin tea - and breakfast is a generous spread in the morning. There is a private toilet tent at camp. Winter nights drop to around 5°C in December and January, so bring warm layers; we handle the rest. The weather guide has month-by-month temperatures.

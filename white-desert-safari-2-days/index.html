Highlights

The moments you will talk about.

  • Crystal Mountain, a ridge of natural quartz in the sand
  • The golden cliffs of Agabat Valley
  • The Black Desert's volcanic hills, then a swim in a cold natural spring
  • Sunset between the white chalk formations
  • Bedouin dinner cooked on the fire, with sweet tea

  • The Mushroom and Chicken Rock, plus sandboarding on the dunes
1

Day 1: Cairo → Bahariya → the White Desert

▾
Morning

We pick you up at your Cairo hotel around 7:00 and drive south toward Bahariya Oasis (4-5 hours with a coffee stop). At the oasis we switch to the 4x4 and have lunch.

Midday

Into the desert: the Black Desert's volcanic hills, then a swim in a cold natural spring hidden in the dunes.

Afternoon

Crystal Mountain and the golden cliffs of Agabat Valley, before the landscape turns white.

Evening

Sunset between the chalk formations. Our Bedouin team sets up camp and cooks dinner on the fire. Then stargazing: no light pollution, just the stars over your tent.

2

Day 2: Sunrise → Mushroom Rock → Cairo

▾
Early morning

Sunrise between the white rocks, breakfast at camp.

Morning

The Mushroom and Chicken Rock and the Valley of the Tents, then sandboarding on the dunes.

Midday

Drive back toward Bahariya, lunch at the oasis.

Evening

Drop-off at your Cairo hotel, around 17:00-18:00.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I book and pay?

Message us on WhatsApp at +20 127 822 0004 and we reply with the price, open dates and everything you need. There is no prepayment and no payment link: you pay after your trip. Cancellation is free.

When does the trip start and end?

We pick you up at your Cairo hotel around 7:00 on day 1 and drop you back there around 17:00-18:00 on day 2.

Is the trip suitable for solo travelers and women?

Yes. Trips run in small groups of 1 to 10 people, and many of our guests are women traveling solo from Europe. You are part of a friendly group the whole way.

What should I bring?

Warm layers for the desert night, sunscreen, sunglasses and a camera. We handle the driving, the camp and the cooking.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, dogs are allowed in the protected area.

Which languages do you speak?

Our team speaks Arabic and English, and we can help in French, Spanish and Italian too.

Ready to sleep under the stars?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the price and the next available dates. Winter dates fill up fast.

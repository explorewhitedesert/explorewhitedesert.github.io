Policy

Booking, payment & cancellation.

Everything about how booking works with us, in one place. Short version: you message us on WhatsApp, you pay nothing before the trip, and you can cancel free at any time.

How to book

Message us on WhatsApp Send us your date and group size at +20 127 822 0004. We reply with the exact price and everything you need, usually within the hour. You talk directly with the team that runs the trip, no middlemen, no account, no forms.

Payment

No prepayment There is no prepayment and no payment link. You pay in cash after your trip finishes, in your currency — we accept cash in all major currencies.
Price: from $165 per person. We keep every trip small and personal, so the exact price depends on your date and group size. Message us for today's price.

Cancellation

Free cancellation Cancellation is free at any time, no questions asked. Just message us on WhatsApp as soon as your plans change.

What's included

4x4 transport from your Cairo hotel and back, one night camping in the White Desert (tents, mattresses, blankets), all meals, water, soft drinks and sweet Bedouin tea, your experienced Bedouin guide and driver, White Desert reserve entry tickets, all stops, and sandboarding on the dunes.

Not included: your hotel in Cairo before or after the trip, extra drinks, tips for the team, and travel insurance.

Questions?

Ask us anything on WhatsApp at +20 127 822 0004. We reply fast, usually within the hour.

White Desert overnight from Cairo: 2 days, 1 night

One night is all it takes. Leave Cairo after breakfast, watch the desert turn from black volcanic hills to white chalk by afternoon, sleep at a Bedouin camp under the stars, and be back at your hotel the next evening.

The two days, hour by hour

Day 1 - Cairo → Bahariya → the White Desert

  • 07:00 · Pickup from your hotel in Cairo, drive south (4-5 hrs with a coffee stop)
  • 11:30 · Bahariya Oasis, switch to the 4x4, lunch
  • 13:00 · Black Desert volcanic hills and the cold spring
  • 15:00 · Crystal Mountain and the golden cliffs of Agabat Valley

Day 2 - Sunrise → Mushroom Rock → Cairo

  • 06:00 · Sunrise between the white rocks, breakfast at camp
  • 08:00 · Mushroom and Chicken Rock, the Valley of the Tents
  • 09:30 · Sandboarding on the dunes
  • 11:00 · Drive back toward Bahariya, lunch at the oasis
  • 17:00 · Drop-off at your Cairo hotel

The night at camp

You sleep in a proper tent with mattresses and thick blankets. Dinner is cooked on the fire - grilled chicken, rice, vegetables, Bedouin tea - and breakfast is a generous spread in the morning. Winter nights drop to around 5°C in December and January, so bring warm layers; we handle the rest. There is a private toilet tent at camp.

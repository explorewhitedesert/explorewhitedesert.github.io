The night

Your first adventure as a married couple.

We take couples from Cairo into the Black Desert, Crystal Mountain and the White Desert National Park with a Bedouin guide. You ride in a 4x4, eat dinner cooked on the fire, and sleep in a tent beside the white rocks. At sunrise the desert turns pink and gold, and it is quiet.

Privacy

Small, private, yours.

Trips run in small groups, and for honeymooners we keep it intimate: your own tent, your own pace, a sunset spot picked for the two of you. Our Bedouin team handles the driving, the camp and the cooking while you watch the moon rise.

What is included

Everything is arranged.

Transfers from Cairo and back, all meals and drinks, protected-area tickets, tent and bedding. You bring warm layers and a camera; we handle the rest. Message us for the price for your dates and group.

What to pack for the White Desert in December

December is our busiest month for a reason: mild sunny days and cold, crystal-clear nights. The trick is that you need very little, because we carry the whole camp, but the few things you do bring matter. Here is the weather as it really is, what we provide, and the short list to pack.

What December is really like out there

Days are sunny and mild, usually around 20-25°C: perfect for the formations and the long drives. When the sun drops, the temperature drops with it. Nights get cold, often below 10°C, and that clear winter sky is exactly why the stars are so good. The swing between day and night is the thing most visitors underestimate.

December 24-26 and New Year's Eve are the first dates to fill every year. If those are your dates, message us early.

Dinner is cooked on the campfire and breakfast is served at camp. You do not need a sleeping bag, a tent or any cooking gear.

The short packing list

  • Warm layers for the night: a fleece or jacket, long trousers and warm socks. December nights are properly cold, and the campfire only warms one side of you.
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat for the day. The winter sun is still desert sun.
  • A camera or a phone you like shooting with. This is the trip you will want it for.
  • Comfortable closed shoes for walking on sand and around the formations.
  • Personal medication and anything you cannot be two days without.
  • A small bag beats a big suitcase for the desert days. If you are traveling with large luggage, ask us on WhatsApp when you book and we will sort it out.

Is December a good time to visit the White Desert?

Yes, it is the heart of the season: mild sunny days around 20-25°C and cold, clear nights. It is also our busiest month, so message us as soon as you know your dates.

How cold does it get at night?

Often below 10°C in December. We provide the tent, mattresses, bedding and plenty of blankets, but bring your own warm layers for the evening.

Do I need to bring a sleeping bag?

No. We provide the tent, mattresses, bedding and blankets. Warm personal layers for the night are all you need.

Do I need camping experience?

None at all. Our Bedouin guides handle the driving, the camp and the cooking. You just bring yourself and your warm layers.

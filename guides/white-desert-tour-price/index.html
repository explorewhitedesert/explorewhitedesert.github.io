Why there is no fixed price list

A White Desert overnight trip is not a bus tour with fixed seats. The cost of a trip depends on your travel date, how many people are in your group, and where in Cairo we pick you up. A single honest number on a website would be wrong for most groups, so we do not publish one. Instead, you tell us your dates and group size, and we send you the exact price for your trip, with everything itemized.

What the price always includes

  • 4x4 transport from Cairo and back, door to door from your hotel or address.
  • Private tent, mattress and blankets for the night in the White Desert.
  • All meals and drinks: lunch on day one, a full Bedouin dinner cooked on the fire, breakfast at sunrise, plus water, soft drinks and tea.
  • A Bedouin guide-driver from our own team, born in Bahariya, who stays with you the whole trip.
  • National park entry tickets for the White Desert.

There are no hidden extras on top. If something would cost more, we tell you before you confirm, never on the road.

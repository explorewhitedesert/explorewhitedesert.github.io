What to pack for a night camping in the White Desert

Explore White Desert · November 2026

You do not need expedition gear. The camping equipment, food and water are all provided. This is the short, honest list of what is actually worth bringing for one night in the Sahara.

What is already provided

On our trips, all camping equipment is included: tents, mattresses and blankets, plus all meals, water, soft drinks and Bedouin tea. There is a fire in the evening and breakfast in the morning. So leave the tent, the sleeping bag debate and the cooler at home.

Clothing: think layers

  • A warm jacket or fleece. This is the one thing people underestimate. From November to February, desert nights can drop close to 0°C. A proper warm layer is the difference between a magical evening by the fire and a long, cold night.
  • Light long trousers and a long-sleeved shirt for the day: sun protection that breathes.
  • A hat, sunglasses and sunscreen. The chalk landscape reflects sunlight from every direction, even in winter.
  • Closed shoes that can handle sand and sharp little stones. Sandals are fine around camp in the evening.
  • A scarf or buff. Useful against both sun and the occasional wind.

Small things that matter

  • A flashlight or headlamp. Once the fire dies down, the desert is very dark.
  • A power bank. There is no electricity in the desert, and phones drain fast in photo mode.
  • Wet wipes and hand sanitizer. There are no bathrooms in the desert; your guide will explain the arrangements.
  • Personal medication, including anything for motion sickness if you are sensitive: part of the route is off-road.
  • A small backpack for the day, so your main bag can stay in the vehicle.
  • Your camera or phone, with space on the memory card. You will take more photos than you expect.

What to leave behind

Valuables, anything that cannot get sandy, and large luggage. Most travelers leave their big suitcases at their Cairo hotel and travel with an overnight bag. If you are moving on directly after the trip, tell us in advance and we will plan the luggage with you.

One last thing

Bring a sense of unhurried time. There is no Wi-Fi, no signal in most of the desert, and that is exactly the point. Dinner by the fire, more stars than you have ever seen, and sunrise over a white landscape: that is what you are packing for.

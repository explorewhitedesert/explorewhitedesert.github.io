How booking a White Desert tour actually works

No booking engine, no card forms, no prepayment. You send us one WhatsApp message, we reply with your exact price and what it covers, you confirm, and we pick you up at your Cairo address on the day. Here is the whole process, step by step.

Step 1: Send us one message

Message us on WhatsApp at +20 127 822 0004 with three things: your travel date (or a rough window), how many people you are, and where in Cairo you are staying. That is all we need to start. Write in whichever language is easiest for you; we will make it work.

Step 2: Get your exact quote

We reply the same day, usually within the hour, with the full price for your group and date, everything itemized: 4x4 transport from Cairo and back, private tent and bedding, all meals and drinks, your Bedouin guide and the national park tickets. If your date is taken, we tell you the nearest open ones. Questions back and forth are normal, ask as many as you like.

Step 3: Confirm with one word

You confirm, and your places are held. There is no payment link, no card details, no deposit. We take you at your word, and you get everything in writing in the chat: the date, the pickup time and place, the price and what it includes.

Step 4: The trip

On day one our driver meets you at your Cairo address, usually early morning. Two days and one night later you are back at the same door by evening: Bahariya, the Black Desert, Crystal Mountain, Agabat Valley, sunset camp in the White Desert, dinner by the fire, a night under the stars, sunrise and the formations.

Step 5: Pay after the trip

You pay in cash after the trip, not before. If anything about the trip was not what we promised, you say so before you pay. We have worked this way since 2001, and it is the simplest guarantee we can give.

The December offer: FLASH15

For December 2026 departures: 15% off with the code FLASH15, first 5 bookings only. Mention FLASH15 in your first message and we apply it to your quote. When the five places are gone, the offer ends.

Do I need to pay anything in advance?

No. There is no deposit, no payment link and no card details. You pay in cash after the trip.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for December, our busiest season. Message us as soon as you know your dates and we will tell you what is open.

What if my plans change?

Just message us as early as you can. Because we take no prepayment, there is nothing to refund and nothing to chase.

Where do you pick us up?

At your hotel or address in Cairo, on the morning of day one, and we bring you back to the same place by the evening of day two.

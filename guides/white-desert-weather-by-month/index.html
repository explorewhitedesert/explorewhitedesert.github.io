White Desert weather by month

Explore White Desert · November 2026

The White Desert is a place of extremes: mild, sunny winter days and genuinely cold nights. Here is what to expect, month by month, so you pack right and pick the dates that suit you.

The short version

October to April is the season: warm, dry, sunny days and cool to cold nights. December and January are the coldest months - comfortable by day, cold enough by night that you will want a real jacket. May to September is hot, with midday temperatures that make camping hard work.

Month by month

  • October: days around 28-30°C, nights around 15°C. Warm and easy, one of the most popular months.
  • November: days around 24-26°C, nights around 10-12°C. Many people's favorite: perfect hiking weather.
  • December: days around 20-22°C, nights can drop to 5-7°C. Bring a warm jacket for the evening.
  • January: the coldest month. Days around 19-21°C, nights around 4-6°C, occasionally colder in a cold snap. We bring thick blankets, and the fire does the rest.
  • February: days around 22-24°C, nights around 6-8°C. Still properly cold after dark.
  • March: days around 26-28°C, nights around 10-12°C. Watch for the khamsin: a hot, dusty wind that can blow for a day or two in spring.
  • April: days around 30-32°C, nights around 13-15°C. The last easy month before summer.
  • May-September: days regularly 35-40°C. Trips are possible but we recommend the winter half of the year.

How cold is "cold", really?

The surprise for most visitors is the night. The desert loses heat fast after sunset, and a January night around 5°C in a sleeping bag feels very different from a European 5°C in a house. It is not dangerous - it is simply cold. With the warm layers we recommend in our packing guide and the blankets we provide, guests sleep comfortably every night.

Rain and wind

Rain is rare - a few days a year at most - but the wind matters more. A breezy evening can make a mild night feel much colder, which is why we pitch camps in sheltered spots among the formations. In spring, the khamsin can bring a day of blowing sand; if the forecast looks bad, we tell you honestly and suggest moving the date.

The honest recommendation

If you can choose freely, come between late October and early April. If you want the desert nearly to yourself and do not mind cold nights, December to February is magical - and the low winter sun makes the formations glow all afternoon.

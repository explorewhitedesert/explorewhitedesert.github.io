The place itself

The White Desert (Sahara el Beyda) is a protected national park between the Bahariya and Farafra oases, about 370 km southwest of Cairo. It is not a conflict zone or an empty wilderness where you are on your own: it is one of Egypt’s established nature destinations, visited in every season. The real risks are practical ones, sun, cold nights, remoteness, and those are exactly what a good operator manages for you.

The honest part: it is remote

There is no phone signal in most of the desert and no shops or clinics around the corner. That is the point of the trip, but it means two things. First, tell someone in Cairo your plans and dates before you go (when you book with us, they can always reach us). Second, bring personal medication and tell us about any condition in advance, so the team is prepared.

The drive from Cairo

The road from Cairo to Bahariya is a normal paved highway, about four hours. The off-road part begins after Bahariya, in our 4x4 vehicles with drivers who do this route constantly. Seatbelts on the highway, steady driving off-road, no rally driving.

Is the White Desert safe for solo women travelers?

Yes, and many of our guests are women traveling alone. You stay with one small team the whole trip and sleep in your own private tent. We are happy to answer specific concerns on WhatsApp before you book.

Is there phone signal in the White Desert?

No, not in most of it. Tell someone your dates before you go; when you travel with us, people in Cairo can always reach our team.

Do I need any desert experience?

None. Our Bedouin team handles the driving, the camp and the cooking. You just bring warm layers for the night.

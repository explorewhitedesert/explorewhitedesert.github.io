December weather in the White Desert

Days are mild and bright, usually 20-22°C with a deep blue sky and almost no humidity. After sunset the temperature drops fast: expect 5-7°C at night, occasionally lower in a cold snap. It almost never rains. The low winter sun makes the white chalk formations glow from mid-afternoon until the light goes - December is the best month of the year for photography.

What the nights feel like

Cold, quiet and full of stars. December nights are properly cold by Egyptian standards, so the evening is built around the fire: dinner cooked fresh at camp, sweet Bedouin tea, and blankets while the sky does its show. In the tents we provide thick mattresses, sleeping bags and extra blankets - guests sleep comfortably every night, but you will want a warm jacket, a hat and closed shoes for the evening.

What to pack for December

  • A real warm jacket for the evening (the kind you would wear on a European autumn night)
  • Long trousers and closed shoes for camp
  • A hat and light gloves for after midnight
  • T-shirts and sunscreen for the sunny afternoons
  • A power bank and a camera - December light is the best of the year

Christmas and New Year in the desert

December 24th and New Year's Eve are the most requested nights of the whole season. Campfire dinner under the stars instead of a hotel buffet - it is the trip people write home about. Is December too cold? The honest answer

No - as long as you pack for it. The cold is dry and still, and the camp setup is built for it. The guests who have the best time are the ones who bring a proper jacket and then spend the whole evening by the fire watching shooting stars. If you prefer warmer nights, October, November, March and April are milder - our best time to visit guide compares every month.

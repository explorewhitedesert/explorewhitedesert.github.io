Best time to visit the White Desert, Egypt

Explore White Desert · November 2026

The White Desert is open all year, but the experience changes completely with the season. Here is what each part of the year actually feels like, so you can pick the right dates.

The short answer

October to April is the season. Days are mild to warm (roughly 15-25°C in midwinter, warmer at the edges of the season), skies are usually clear, and camping is comfortable with proper blankets. May to September is the hot months: daytime temperatures in the Western Desert regularly pass 35°C, and most travelers find a night in the open desert more exhausting than magical.

November to April: the high season

This is when the White Desert is at its best. Daytime is pleasant for walking among the chalk formations, and the light at sunrise and sunset is soft and golden. Nights are genuinely cold: from December to February, temperatures can drop close to freezing once the sun goes down. Good operators provide thick blankets and a fire, and a warm sleeping setup makes it one of the most memorable nights you can have in Egypt.

Christmas, New Year and European school holidays are the busiest weeks of the year. If you want those dates, book early: guides, drivers and the best camping spots fill up weeks ahead.

May to September: the hot months

Trips still run in summer, and some travelers enjoy having the desert almost to themselves. But expect serious heat in the middle of the day, shorter time outside the vehicle, and warm nights. If summer is your only window, it is still possible, just plan around the heat: early starts, long midday breaks, light clothing.

Moon phases and stargazing

The White Desert has almost no light pollution, which makes it one of the best stargazing spots in Egypt. Around the new moon the sky is at its darkest and the Milky Way is clearly visible on dry, clear nights. Around the full moon the landscape itself glows almost white, which is beautiful in a different way, but the fainter stars wash out. If stargazing is your priority, check the moon calendar when choosing your dates.

Wind and the khamsin

In spring (roughly March and April) Egypt sometimes gets the khamsin, a hot, dry wind that can carry sand for a day or two. It is not dangerous, but it can reduce visibility and make camping dusty. Guides track the forecast and adjust plans when needed.

So when should you go?

  • For the classic experience: November to February.
  • For fewer people with still-good weather: October, March and early April.
  • For stargazing: aim for the nights around a new moon.
  • For Christmas and New Year: book well in advance.

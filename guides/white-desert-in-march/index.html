White Desert in March

March weather in the White Desert

March is the start of the mild shoulder season, and for many guests the sweet spot. Days are warm, usually 23-26°C in the sun, and nights cool rather than cold: expect 8-12°C after sunset. The sky stays clear and rain is still almost unheard of. Windy afternoons become more common toward the end of the month - the spring khamaseen winds can bring a dusty day or two.

What the nights feel like

Mild enough to sit outside for hours, cool enough to enjoy the campfire. Dinner is cooked fresh at camp, the Bedouin tea keeps coming, and the stars are out all night. We provide thick mattresses, sleeping bags and blankets - in March most guests sleep under one blanket and call it their favourite night in Egypt.

What to pack for March

  • A light warm jacket or fleece for the evening - no heavy winter coat needed
  • Long trousers and closed shoes for camp
  • T-shirts, sunglasses and sunscreen for the warm afternoons
  • A scarf for the occasional windy, dusty afternoon
  • A power bank and a camera

March or winter - which is better?

December to February give you the coldest nights and the clearest skies; March gives you warmer evenings and longer light. If you want the full campfire-and-frost experience, pick winter. If you want to linger outside after dinner without a heavy jacket, pick March. Our best time to visit guide compares every month.

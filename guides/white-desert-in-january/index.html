White Desert in January

Explore White Desert · November 2026

January is the coldest and clearest month in the White Desert: crisp sunny days, frosty starry nights, and the best stargazing of the whole season. Here is exactly what to expect.

January weather in the White Desert

January is the coldest month of the desert year, and for many guests the best one. Days are sunny and mild, usually around 20°C with a clear blue sky. After sunset the temperature drops quickly: expect 5-7°C at night, and colder in a snap - frost on the tent is possible before sunrise. Rain is almost unheard of. The air is at its clearest in January, which is why photographers and stargazers love it.

What the nights feel like

Properly cold, completely quiet and full of stars. The evening is built around the campfire: dinner cooked fresh at camp, sweet Bedouin tea, and blankets while the sky does its show. In the tents we provide thick mattresses, sleeping bags and extra blankets - guests sleep comfortably every night, but a warm jacket, a hat and closed shoes are essential for the evening.

What to pack for January

  • A real winter jacket for the evening - the coldest nights of the season are in January
  • Long trousers, closed shoes and warm socks for camp
  • A hat and light gloves for after midnight
  • T-shirts, sunglasses and sunscreen for the sunny afternoons
  • A power bank and a camera - the January sky is the clearest of the year

Is January too cold? The honest answer

No - as long as you pack for it. The cold is dry and still, and the camp setup is built for it. The guests who have the best time bring a proper jacket and spend the whole evening by the fire. If you prefer warmer nights, October, November, March and April are milder - our best time to visit guide compares every month.

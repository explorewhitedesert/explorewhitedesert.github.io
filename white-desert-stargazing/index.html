White Desert stargazing: a night under the Milky Way

No city glow, no noise, no schedule. One night in the White Desert gives you the darkest sky most Europeans have ever seen - and a comfortable camp to enjoy it from.

What you see

The Milky Way as a bright band overhead, planets, and shooting stars most nights. On moonless weeks the sky is so dark that the white chalk formations shine by starlight alone. On full-moon nights the desert glows like snow - a different, equally surreal show. Our stargazing calendar lists the best weekends of the season.

The trip

You leave Cairo in the morning, cross the Black Desert and Agabat Valley by 4x4, and reach the white chalk formations by afternoon. Sunset, a fire-cooked Bedouin dinner, then the sky. You sleep in a proper tent with mattresses and thick blankets, and wake to sunrise over the formations before the drive back.

What is included

  • 4x4 transport from your Cairo hotel and back
  • One night of camping: tent, mattresses, blankets
  • All meals, water, soft drinks and Bedouin tea
  • Experienced Bedouin guide and driver
  • White Desert reserve tickets

Good to know

Winter nights are cold - around 5°C in December and January - so bring warm layers; we handle the rest. Photographers: a phone with night mode captures the Milky Way here. See the photography guide and our night sky story.

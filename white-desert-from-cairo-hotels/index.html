How it works from your hotel

  1. Day 1, 07:00: pickup at your Cairo hotel, 4-5 hours south with a coffee stop.
  2. 11:30: Bahariya Oasis, switch to the 4x4, lunch at the oasis.
  3. Afternoon: Black Desert, cold spring, Crystal Mountain, Agabat Valley, sunset between the chalk formations.
  4. Evening: Bedouin dinner by the fire, stargazing, sleep in camp. Day 2: sunrise, Mushroom Rock, sandboarding, back at your hotel around 17:00.

What is included

  • 4x4 transport from your Cairo hotel and back
  • One night of camping: tent, mattresses, blankets
  • All meals, water, soft drinks and Bedouin tea
  • Experienced Bedouin guide and driver
  • White Desert reserve tickets

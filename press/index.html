Explore White Desert: press kit

Everything a journalist, blogger or podcast host needs to cover the White Desert, Egypt, from the people who run the trips. Use the photos free with credit and a link to this site. Questions answered fast on email or WhatsApp.

The operator

Explore White Desert is run by Kimo Nasr from Bawiti, in Bahariya Oasis, the gateway to Egypt's White Desert. The team runs Bedouin-guided overnight safaris from Cairo: 4x4 across the Black Desert, Crystal Mountain and Agabat Valley, then camp beside the chalk formations, dinner cooked on the fire, sunrise over a landscape that looks like snow in the middle of the Sahara.

Guests deal directly with the people who run the trip. Trips are led by Bedouin guides.

Story angles

  • "This is Egypt, not Antarctica": the chalk desert that looks like snow, a few hours southwest of Cairo.
  • A winter-sun escape for Europeans: mild 20-25°C days and cold, star-filled nights from November to April.
  • Bedouin-guided camping: dinner on the fire, tea, no hotel, no crowds.
  • Beyond the pyramids: a one-night add-on for travelers already in Cairo.
  • Photography: wind-carved formations, golden-hour silhouettes and starry skies.
  • Small-group, locally run desert tourism.

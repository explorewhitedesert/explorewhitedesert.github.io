White Desert with kids: what a family trip is really like

Explore White Desert · November 2026

Sandboarding down dunes, sleeping in a tent, dinner from a real fire, and a sky full of stars: for children the White Desert is not a sightseeing stop, it is the adventure of the whole holiday.

What ages does it suit?

We regularly take families with children from about 5 years up. The drives between stops are broken up with short walks and sand play, so younger kids do not get restless. Teenagers usually declare it the best day of the Egypt trip - sandboarding helps.

How the night works with children

The camp is set up before dark, tents have proper mattresses and thick blankets, and dinner is early enough for young sleepers. Most kids are asleep within minutes after a day in the fresh air. Winter nights are cold, so pack warm layers for the children - our packing list has a family section. Toilets at camp are simple but clean and private.

What kids love most

  • Sandboarding and rolling down the dunes at Agabat Valley
  • Finding the "mushroom" and "chicken" rocks - a natural treasure hunt
  • Helping with the campfire and sweet Bedouin tea
  • Spotting shooting stars before bed

Safety, honestly

You are with an experienced Bedouin driver and guide the whole time, in a well-maintained 4x4, with water, first aid and satellite communication on board. The desert is remote - that is the point - and we plan for it. Tell us the ages of your children when you message us and we will shape the stops around them.

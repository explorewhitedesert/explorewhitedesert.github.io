New Year's Eve in the White Desert: a fire, the stars, and silence

Explore White Desert · November 2026

No fireworks, no traffic, no overpriced set menus. Midnight in the White Desert is a fire crackling, sweet tea in your hands, and a sky so full of stars it takes a minute to find the constellations you know.

What New Year here actually looks like

You arrive with the afternoon light, watch the sun drop behind the chalk formations, and sit down to a holiday dinner cooked on the fire. At midnight there is no countdown screen - just the Milky Way, the people you came with, and the first quiet minutes of the new year in the middle of nowhere. Guests tell us it is the first New Year's Eve they actually remember.

The practical side

It is the same 2-day / 1-night program from Cairo, timed for December 31. Nights are cold, around 5°C, so the fire and the blankets matter - pack a warm jacket and we handle everything else. New Year dates are the most requested of the season and groups stay small, so message us as early as you can. Details on the New Year trip page and the Christmas page.

Start the year with the sunrise

The best part is January 1st itself: you wake up in the desert, climb a small ridge with hot tea, and watch the first sunrise of the year turn the white chalk pink. Hard to beat as a way to begin a year.

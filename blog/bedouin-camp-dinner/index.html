How dinner works

While you watch the sunset from the formations, the team prepares dinner the traditional way: meat cooked over the fire, rice, soup and fresh vegetables, finished with sweet Bedouin tea brewed on the coals. Everything is carried in fresh and cooked at camp - nothing is pre-packaged.

After dinner

The evening slows down on purpose. The fire stays lit, the tea keeps coming, and the conversation moves to the sky. There is no program and no schedule - that is the luxury. If you are lucky, your guide will tell stories about growing up in the oasis.
